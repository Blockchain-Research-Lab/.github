# 🚀 Blockchain Research Lab Centre of Excellence (BRLCoE) – AKGEC

> **Founded:** 2019  
> **Website:** [brlakgec.com](https://brlakgec.com/)  
> **Motto:** *Develop. Deploy. Distribute.*

The **Blockchain Research Lab Centre of Excellence (BRLCoE)** is the dedicated research and development centre of **Ajay Kumar Garg Engineering College (AKGEC)**.  
Our mission is to bring **maximum transparency into systems** by building, deploying, and distributing cutting-edge solutions based on **Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT)** and its side technologies.

In collaboration with **DLT Labs**, a pioneer in enterprise blockchain solutions, BRL has successfully delivered impactful projects and fostered innovation across industries.

---

## 📌 Our Strategy

1. **DEVELOP** – Create applications using DLT and supporting technologies.  
2. **DEPLOY** – Offer modern containerized solutions for seamless, scalable services.  
3. **DISTRIBUTE** – Connect organizations with transparent and integrable solutions.
